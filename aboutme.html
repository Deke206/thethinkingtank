    I have been responsible for the whole job before.

    I previously owned and operated a limo and town-car business, which meant handling customers, scheduling, sales, presentation, marketing and vehicle problems. I also have experience around property management, Airbnb operations and automotive work.

    I learned Photoshop, Illustrator, web work and technical troubleshooting because business owners often have to solve those problems themselves.
