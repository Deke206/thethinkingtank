How LED Sim Auto works

Choose Exterior or Interior, then tap either a zone name or the matching shape directly on a vehicle view. Selected SVG zones glow over the unchanged sedan image. Preview hides the available-zone outlines so only your choices remain visible. Continue carries the exact zone IDs into the installation request.
